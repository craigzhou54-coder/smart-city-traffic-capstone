## Recommendation Logic
Historical traffic observations were grouped by hour and ranked according to average traffic volume.
The three lowest-congestion periods were selected as recommended travel windows.

# Example Recommendation
For flexible journeys, consider travelling between 01:00 and 03:59, when historical traffic volumes are typically lower

Avoid peak congestion periods between 15:00 and 17:00

# Value
The recommendation system transforms analytical and machine-learning findings into practial travel guidance for road users and smart-city mobility teams.
