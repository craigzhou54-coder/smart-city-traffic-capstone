# part 1 Findings

## Dateset Overview

The dataset contains 48,204 traffic records.

Date Range:
2012-10-02 to 2018-09-30

## Weather Analysis

Average traffic volume by weather condition:

- Clouds: 3618
- Haze: 3502
- Rain: 3318
- Drizzle: 3291
- Smoke: 3238
- Clear: 3056
- Snow: 3017
- Thunderstorm: 3002
- Mist: 2933
- Fog: 2704
- Squall: 2062

## Insight

Cloudy conditions showed the highest average traffic volume.

Traffic volume appears more influenced by commuting behaviour than by weather conditions alone.

## Yearly Analysis

Average traffic volume by years:
- 2012: 3208
- 2013: 3287
- 2014: 3251
- 2015: 3243
- 2016: 3169
- 2017: 3341
- 2018: 3260

## Insight

Traffic volume remained relatively stable from 2012 to 2018 with moderate fluctuations.

The peak hour was at 16:00 of 5664
The weekday traffic volume was 3534 and the weekend traffic volume was 2571 in average.
